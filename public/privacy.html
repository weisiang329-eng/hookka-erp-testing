Privacy Policy

Hookka ERP & Hookka Worker Portal · Last updated 28 June 2026

These apps are operated by Hookka Industries Sdn Bhd (Company No. 202501060540 / 1661946-X) ("Hookka", "we"). They are internal business tools for Hookka staff and shop-floor workers to manage manufacturing operations (sales, production, delivery, inventory, payroll) and to clock in and scan job cards. This policy explains what we collect and how we use it.

Who can use these apps

Access requires a Hookka-issued account (office staff: email + password) or employee number + PIN (shop-floor workers). The apps are not intended for the general public; without valid Hookka credentials no personal data can be entered or viewed.

Information we collect

  • Account & identity — work email, display name, role (office); employee number and a numeric PIN (workers). Passwords/PINs are stored only as salted hashes, never in plain text.
  • Attendance (Worker Portal) — clock-in/out times and, when you punch, a photo from the device camera (to prevent buddy-punching) and your approximate location (a soft geofence to flag off-site punches). The camera and location are used only at the moment you punch; we do not track location in the background.
  • Business records you enter or view — sales/purchase/delivery orders, invoices, inventory, production and payroll data belonging to Hookka.
  • Technical data — standard request logs (e.g. timestamps, error logs) needed to operate and secure the service.

How we use it

Solely to provide and secure the apps: authenticating you, recording attendance and work, and running Hookka's manufacturing operations. We do not sell your data, do not use it for advertising, and do not share it with third parties except the infrastructure providers that host the service on our behalf (e.g. Cloudflare for hosting, Supabase for the database), under their data-processing terms.

Data retention & security

Records are retained for as long as needed for business and legal purposes and then deleted or anonymised. Data is transmitted over HTTPS and access is restricted to authenticated users by role.
